# Article figures

Figures for the two articles about this project on Medium (Towards AI). The text lives on
Medium; only the images are kept here.

- [`figures-01/`](figures-01/): *How to stop an AI agent from giving away your money in a
  negotiation*. Charts generated from the attack reports by `src/make_figures.py` (SVG) and
  `src/render.js` (PNG at 2x), plus screenshots of the demo.
- [`figures-02/`](figures-02/): *What it takes to build a voice agent that can hold a phone
  call*. Diagrams designed in Figma and exported at 2x, plus a screenshot of the call screen.

All figures are by the author, Juan Sebastián Peña Donneys, and are under the repository's
MIT license.
